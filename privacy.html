Symplifi Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026 · Provider: The Little Byte LLC · Contact: willie@thelittlebyte.com

Symplifi is a private journal for organizing your physical state, symptoms, notes, and medical records. This policy explains the information the app handles, why it is used, the services that process it, and the controls available to you.

Information Symplifi handles

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address.
  • Health journal content. This includes the observations you record, cleaned note text, daily summaries, medical records and extracted text, questions and answers in Ask Anything, and images you choose to attach to an Ask conversation. Ask stores a bounded JPEG copy rather than the original camera or photo-library asset.
  • Legacy DogLog Archive. If you used DogLog, your existing animal-health history remains available to you in a separate read-only archive. Archive content is not used in Symplifi summaries, search, Ask context, model prompts, or human-health analytics.
  • Device and service information. We may process an Apple Push Notification service token if you enable notifications, purchase and entitlement metadata associated with the existing App Store product, and limited operational and usage metadata described below.

Audio

When you record a note, transcription occurs on your device using Apple's speech recognition. The audio is not uploaded or stored by Symplifi. Only the resulting text is saved to your private journal.

How we use information

We use your information to authenticate you; store, display, and delete your journal; transcribe notes; clean up text; generate summaries; extract information from records; answer your questions; provide requested web research; operate notifications and purchases; prevent abuse; diagnose failures; and maintain the security of the service.

Symplifi does not sell your information and does not use health content for advertising.

Services that process information

We use the following service providers only to operate Symplifi:

  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, on-device speech recognition, App Store purchases, and push notifications.
  • Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, private file storage, and server functions. Human-health data is restricted to the signed-in owner.
  • Google Gemini processes note text, medical-record files (including uploaded PDFs) and extracted record text, Ask questions, relevant journal context, and images you attach when needed to provide the AI features you request.
  • Exa may process an Ask question to research a fixed allowlist of reputable human-health sources. Research results are used only for that Ask response.
  • PostHog receives metadata-only product and AI telemetry as described below.
  • RevenueCat may receive an account identifier and App Store purchase or entitlement metadata to preserve purchase continuity. It does not receive your health journal or archive content.

These providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they operate, subject to their own security and privacy commitments.

Metadata-only analytics boundary

Symplifi uses PostHog for product analytics and AI reliability monitoring. PostHog may receive bounded metadata such as feature and screen identifiers, timestamps, counts, lengths, booleans, bounded status or error categories, token counts, latency, and estimated cost, associated with an account identifier.

PostHog does not receive health note text, record content, Ask questions or answers, summaries, images, audio, prompts, model output, web excerpts, file or Storage paths, or Legacy DogLog Archive content. Symplifi does not use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or session replay.

AI health information and emergencies

Symplifi's AI features organize information and provide general educational information. They do not provide medical care, diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, recommend individualized dosing, or monitor emergencies. AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated. Check important information with a qualified healthcare professional.

For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, call 911 or your local emergency number. Do not rely on Symplifi to assess or monitor an emergency.

Privacy and security

Human logs, summaries, records, Ask history, images, and stored files are isolated from other accounts using database row-level security and private Storage policies. Legacy animal data is kept in a separate read-only path and is not available to active Symplifi AI or analytics.

Information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Server credentials and AI provider keys are not included in the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Symplifi is a consumer app offered directly to individuals. We do not claim that Symplifi is HIPAA compliant or that The Little Byte LLC is acting as a HIPAA covered entity or business associate.

Retention and deletion

We retain your account and content while your account exists and as needed to operate the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, or protect the service.

You can delete an individual human-health record or your human journal in the app. You can also delete your account from the app. Deletion removes associated private files before database rows and is designed to resume safely if interrupted. Account deletion also requests revocation of your Sign in with Apple token.

If you are the sole remaining member of a former DogLog household, account deletion permanently deletes that household's animal-health evidence and stored record objects. If another authorized member remains, account deletion does not delete animal-health history that belongs to that other member. In either case, your own access to the Legacy DogLog Archive ends when your account is deleted. Some limited security, deletion-receipt, or legal records may be retained when reasonably necessary and permitted by law.

Your choices

You may choose whether to provide optional name and email information through Apple, whether to enable notifications, whether to attach images, and whether to use AI and web-research features. You may review and delete supported content in the app or delete your account.

Children

Symplifi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will revise the effective date above and provide notice in the app when a material change requires it.
