Symplifi Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026 · Provider: The Little Byte LLC · Contact: willie@thelittlebyte.com

Symplifi is a private journal for organizing your physical state, symptoms, notes, and medical records. This policy explains the information the app handles, why it is used, the services that process it, and the controls available to you.

Information Symplifi handles

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address.
  • Health journal content. This includes the observations you record, cleaned note text, daily summaries, medical records and extracted text, questions and answers in Ask Anything, and images you choose to attach to an Ask conversation. Ask stores a bounded JPEG copy rather than the original camera or photo-library asset.
  • Optional Apple Health information. If you explicitly connect Apple Health, Symplifi requests read-only access to sleep analysis; steps; active energy; exercise time; workouts; resting heart rate; heart-rate variability (HRV SDNN); respiratory rate; and sleeping wrist temperature. Raw HealthKit samples, sample identifiers, source-app information, and device identifiers stay on your iPhone. Symplifi stores only deterministic daily aggregates, the applicable local date and time-zone identifier, and sync metadata in your private Supabase account.
  • Legacy DogLog Archive. If you used DogLog, your existing animal-health history remains available to you in a separate read-only archive. Archive content is not used in Symplifi summaries, search, Ask context, model prompts, or human-health analytics.
  • Device and service information. We may process an Apple Push Notification service token if you enable notifications, purchase and entitlement metadata associated with the existing App Store product, and limited operational and usage metadata described below.

Audio

When you record a note, transcription occurs on your device using Apple's speech recognition. The audio is not uploaded or stored by Symplifi. Only the resulting text is saved to your private journal.

How we use information

We use your information to authenticate you; store, display, and delete your journal; transcribe notes; clean up text; generate summaries; extract information from records; answer your questions; provide requested web research; operate notifications and purchases; prevent abuse; diagnose failures; and maintain the security of the service.

Symplifi does not sell your information and does not use health content for advertising.

Services that process information

We use the following service providers only to operate Symplifi:

  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, on-device speech recognition, optional read-only HealthKit access, App Store purchases, and push notifications.
  • Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, private file storage, and server functions. Human-health data is restricted to the signed-in owner.
  • Google Gemini processes note text, medical-record files (including uploaded PDFs) and extracted record text, Ask questions, relevant journal context, and images you attach when needed to provide the AI features you request. Apple Health daily aggregates are sent to Gemini only when you request an Ask response that uses them or when Symplifi generates the existing Daily Summary for a day that already has one of your journal entries. Apple Health data alone never triggers a Daily Summary or Gemini call.
  • Exa may process a generic health topic derived from an Ask request to research a fixed allowlist of reputable human-health sources. Exact personal Apple Health measurements and dates are never sent to Exa. Research results are used only for that Ask response.
  • PostHog receives metadata-only product and AI telemetry as described below.
  • RevenueCat may receive an account identifier and App Store purchase or entitlement metadata to preserve purchase continuity. It does not receive your health journal or archive content.

These providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they operate, subject to their own security and privacy commitments.

Metadata-only analytics boundary

Symplifi uses PostHog for product analytics and AI reliability monitoring. PostHog may receive bounded metadata such as feature and screen identifiers, timestamps, counts, lengths, booleans, bounded status or error categories, token counts, latency, and estimated cost, associated with an account identifier.

PostHog does not receive health note text, record content, Ask questions or answers, summaries, images, audio, prompts, model output, web excerpts, file or Storage paths, or Legacy DogLog Archive content. For Apple Health sync, PostHog may receive only coarse booleans, bounded counts, latency, and allowlisted generic error categories. It does not receive Apple Health values, dates, sleep intervals, per-category permission information, source or device information, provenance, or sync payload content. Symplifi does not use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or session replay.

Optional Apple Health connection

Connecting Apple Health is voluntary and is not required to use the rest of Symplifi. Before Apple's permission sheet appears, Symplifi explains the requested categories, the initial import, ongoing sync, private storage, and AI processing described in this policy. Symplifi asks only for read access and never writes to Apple Health.

If you connect, Symplifi imports up to the trailing 366 authorized local dates and can continue refreshing in the foreground and through Apple Health background delivery. It stores nullable daily totals or averages for the approved categories, sleep interval and available stage aggregates, aggregate workout count, duration, energy, and activity types, along with local date, time-zone identifier, aggregation version, and server timestamps. It does not upload or store raw samples, sample UUIDs, source apps, device identifiers, or individual workout or sleep intervals. Stored history can grow through ongoing sync and is retained until you disconnect Apple Health, delete your journal, or delete your account.

HealthKit does not tell an app whether you denied a particular read category. Symplifi therefore does not claim that an individual category was granted or denied. A category with no available data may be denied, unavailable, outside the authorized history, or simply have no recorded data. Missing measurements are treated as unknown, not as zero or proof that something did not happen.

Apple Health measurements remain separate from observations you write. They may enrich Ask and the existing Daily Summary, with Apple Health identified as the source. A day containing only Apple Health measurements gets no summary and causes no Gemini request. Symplifi may describe recorded associations but does not use Apple Health for diagnosis, treatment direction, threshold-based alerts, or emergency monitoring.

One server-issued, private connection credential determines which connected device may upload for the account at a time. Connecting or reconnecting on another device replaces that credential so older uploaders fail closed. The server stores only a one-way hash of the credential and does not store a HealthKit or installation device identifier.

Disconnecting in Symplifi stops future uploads and deletes all imported Apple Health daily aggregates from your account. It does not revoke Apple's HealthKit permission; revoke that separately in the Health app or iOS Settings if desired. Disconnecting or deleting your journal or account also clears the corresponding local sync state. Signing out stops observers and uploads, and another account must separately consent.

AI health information and emergencies

Symplifi's AI features organize information and provide general educational information. They do not provide medical care, diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, recommend individualized dosing, or monitor emergencies. AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated. Check important information with a qualified healthcare professional.

For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, call 911 or your local emergency number. Do not rely on Symplifi to assess or monitor an emergency.

Privacy and security

Human logs, summaries, records, Ask history, images, and stored files are isolated from other accounts using database row-level security and private Storage policies. Legacy animal data is kept in a separate read-only path and is not available to active Symplifi AI or analytics.

Information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Server credentials and AI provider keys are not included in the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Symplifi is a consumer app offered directly to individuals. We do not claim that Symplifi is HIPAA compliant or that The Little Byte LLC is acting as a HIPAA covered entity or business associate.

Retention and deletion

We retain your account and content while your account exists and as needed to operate the service, comply with law, resolve disputes, or protect the service.

You can delete an individual human-health record or your human journal in the app. You can also delete your account from the app. Journal or account deletion removes the Apple Health connection, stored daily aggregates, and corresponding local sync state. Deletion removes associated private files before database rows and is designed to resume safely if interrupted. Account deletion also requests revocation of your Sign in with Apple token.

If you are the sole remaining member of a former DogLog household, account deletion permanently deletes that household's animal-health evidence and stored record objects. If another authorized member remains, account deletion does not delete animal-health history that belongs to that other member. In either case, your own access to the Legacy DogLog Archive ends when your account is deleted. Some limited security, deletion-receipt, or legal records may be retained when reasonably necessary and permitted by law.

Your choices

You may choose whether to provide optional name and email information through Apple, whether to enable notifications, whether to attach images, whether to connect Apple Health, and whether to use AI and web-research features. You may review and delete supported content in the app, disconnect Apple Health, or delete your journal or account.

Children

Symplifi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
